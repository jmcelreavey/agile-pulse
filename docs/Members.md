# Members

Members in Agile Pulse represent individual team members who participate in sprints and are assigned tasks. They are unique to a user domain and play a crucial role in sprint planning and execution.

## Key Features

### Member Properties

- Members are unique within a domain
- Members can be assigned an email address (must be unique within the domain)
- Members can only exist once per sprint chart

### Member Management

- Members can be added, edited, and removed from sprints
- Members can be archived

## User Interactions

### Adding and Editing Members

- Users can add new members to a domain
- Existing member details can be edited

### Assigning Members to Sprints

- Members can be added to or removed from specific sprints

### Assigning Tasks

- Tasks can be assigned to members within a sprint

### Archiving Members

- Members can be archived when they are no longer active
