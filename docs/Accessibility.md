# Accessibility

Agile Pulse aims to work with a keyboard, a screen reader and either colour scheme.

## Moving tasks with the keyboard

Every task on the chart can be reached and moved without a mouse.

1. Press **Tab** to move between tasks. The focused task has a yellow outline.
2. Press **Space** to pick it up.
3. Use **Left** and **Right** to move it along the lane, and **Up** and **Down** to move it to another person.
4. Press **Space** to drop it, or **Escape** to cancel.
5. Press **Enter** on a task to open its edit form.

Screen readers announce each task's name, estimate and state, and read out the move instructions and each step of a move.

## Dark mode

Agile Pulse follows your system's light or dark setting by default. Change it from the user menu, or with the sun and moon button when signed out; your choice is remembered on that device.

## Other details

- Icon-only buttons, such as the theme switch, documentation and remove buttons, have labels for screen readers.
- Tables in the insights panels scroll sideways on small screens instead of squashing.
- A task's state is part of its accessible name and its hover card, so it doesn't rely on colour alone.

## Why

Drag and drop is the heart of the chart, so it has to work for people who can't use a mouse. Agile Pulse uses a drag and drop library with built-in keyboard and screen-reader support rather than adding a separate set of buttons.
